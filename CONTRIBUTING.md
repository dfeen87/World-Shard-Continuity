# Contributing

Thank you for contributing to World Shard Continuity. This repository focuses on engine-agnostic continuity contracts, reference architectures, and safety patterns for long-lived worlds.

## Scope Guardrails

Contributions must keep this project focused on the continuity layer only.

Do not add:

- real-money systems;
- pricing, valuation, exchange-rate, or revenue logic;
- marketplaces, auctions, trading venues, or order books;
- payment rails, checkout flows, wallet integrations, or settlement systems;
- compliance logic such as tax, KYC, AML, sanctions, chargebacks, or reporting;
- game-specific mechanics, balancing rules, reward formulas, quests, or progression systems.

Acceptable contributions should stay within continuity concerns such as identity stability, ownership records, transition safety, shard metadata, idempotency, deterministic replay, auditability, escrow-style safety, and future-proof contracts.
